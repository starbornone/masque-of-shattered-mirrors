# One-player, one-GM rehearsal

## Before the scene

- [ ] **Player** — Confirm the starting level with the GM, then use **Download for GM** to send your character file.
- [ ] **GM** — Open the file, edit the tailoring brief, and export a GM package.
- [ ] **Player** — Make one recognizable character change and send the revised file.
- [ ] **GM** — Reopen the package and choose **Update player character**. Confirm the preview shows the expected change, apply it, then export and reopen the updated package. Check that it contains the revised character and your exact edited notes.
- [ ] **Player** — Approve a question for **The mask slips**. Print the table card with the prompt on the reverse and keep it within reach.

## During the scene

- [ ] **Player** — On your phone, open **At the table** and choose **Prepare for offline play**. Once it’s ready, turn on airplane mode and refresh. Confirm that your active draft reopens in Table View.
- [ ] **GM** — At two separate moments, ask for the player’s **Diplomacy modifier** and **Thievery training rank**. Time each from the request to the correct answer. Aim for **five seconds or less per lookup**.
- [ ] **GM** — Give the player a moment when their mask’s lie could be challenged. Let the scene play out without mentioning the prompt. Record whether they open it on their phone, use the printed card, or pass over it.

## After the scene

- [ ] **Together** — Record both lookup times, what happened with the mask prompt, and the biggest interruption to fix next.
